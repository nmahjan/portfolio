Privacy Notice

How information submitted through this portfolio is handled

Information I Collect

When you use the contact form, I receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your first and last name, email address, phone number, inquiry subject, and message. This static website does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

How the Contact Form Works

The contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form delivery provider. When you submit it, your information is transmitted to and processed by Formspree before being delivered to me. Formspree may process technical information needed to operate and protect the form, such as an IP address and request metadata, under its own privacy practices.

How I Use Information

I use contact information only to review your inquiry, respond to you, discuss opportunities or collaborations, prevent misuse of the form, and keep reasonable records of our correspondence. I do not sell or rent your personal information and do not use contact-form submissions for automated decision-making.

Retention and Sharing

I keep messages only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain relevant correspondence, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the contact form, when required by law, or when necessary to protect rights and security.

Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate for a personal portfolio, including encrypted HTTPS delivery and restrictive browser security controls. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please do not submit confidential or highly sensitive information through the form.

Your Choices

You may contact me directly by email instead of using the form. You may also ask what contact information I hold about you or request correction or deletion, subject to any information I must retain for legal or legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email nm.oh.usa@gmail.com. This notice may be updated when the website or its services change.
